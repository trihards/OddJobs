CAR 01 / 03OIL CHECK
Pull the yellow handle up.CHECK 1 / 3
Pull the dipstick, wipe it, reinsert, and read the oil level. If it is below MIN, drag the small oil jug from the upper-left corner to the orange OIL filler cap and hold there to pour. Release, then reinsert the dipstick and check again. The yellow handle is draggable; wiping is done directly on the raised dipstick. When oil is low, drag the jug onto the orange cap and hold to pour, then reinsert and check again. Choose Finish check or Flag overfill only when you're ready to move on. The complete report comes after the third car.

A small arcade puzzle, not an actual car-maintenance guide. Please do not diagnose your real engine with a cartoon.
