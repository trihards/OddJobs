Make a lovely pour-over.

First, give the grounds a little water to bloom. Then move your finger in gentle circles to pour the rest. Lift your finger whenever the water gets too high.

Wake up those grounds.

Press and hold to add about 40g of water. Release when you're happy.

A little water goes a long way.

Now, the slow circle.

Drag your finger around the dotted ring on the coffee bed. Lift to pause and let the water drain. Get to 250g without flooding the filter.

Touch the coffee grounds to start pouring.
